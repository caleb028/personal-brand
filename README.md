# Instaweb Solutions Ltd 🚀
### Professional Software Development & Digital Solutions

## 🌐 Website Pages & Structure

1. **Homepage (`/`)** — Sophisticated hero section with live enterprise software preview (`SoftwareDashboardPreview`), core commitments, 6 services, featured case studies, 5-stage timeline overview, technology matrix, and client inquiry CTA.
2. **About (`/about`)** — Authentic founder story, engineering standards, and the 6 principles of "How I Approach Software Development".
3. **Services (`/services`)** — Detailed specifications, deliverables, business values, and FAQs for all 6 core services:
   - 01. Website Design & Development
   - 02. Custom Web Applications
   - 03. E-Commerce Development
   - 04. Business Management Systems
   - 05. API & Systems Integration
   - 06. Website Maintenance & Technical Support
4. **Portfolio (`/projects`)** — Comprehensive portfolio showcase featuring real systems with problem statements, solutions, technologies, and verified outcomes.
5. **Case Studies (`/projects/[slug]`)** — Deep architectural case studies:
   - `shulebase-kenya` (Comprehensive School Management & CBC Operations System)
   - `top-grade-rice-millers` (Agribusiness Grain Inventory & Milling Traceability System)
   - `rent-ledger` (Multi-Property Rental Management & Tenant Financial Ledger Platform)
   - `smm-kenya` (High-Concurrency Digital Marketing Service Fulfillment Engine)
   - `landconnect-ke` (Land Parcel Verification & Documentation Tracking Platform)
6. **Development Process (`/process`)** — 5-stage engineering lifecycle (Discovery, Planning, Design, Development & Testing, Launch & Support) + 6-step client engagement framework.
7. **Insights & Blog (`/insights`)** — Practical engineering perspectives with search/categories.
8. **Article Case Studies (`/insights/[slug]`)** — Deep technical articles including:
   - *Designing Resilient M-Pesa Payment Handlers in Production*
   - *Why Most Custom Business Software Fails (And How Scope Discipline Fixes It)*
   - *Moving from Excel Sheets to Custom Web Portals: What SMEs Must Plan For*
   - *Balancing Speed and Long-Term Maintainability in Next.js Applications*
9. **Contact (`/contact`)** — Validated project inquiry portal with honeypot spam protection, direct WhatsApp Business click-to-chat integration, and server-side submission handler (`/api/contact`).
10. **Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)** — Transparent data stewardship, confidentiality, and NDA commitments.
11. **Terms of Service (`/terms`)** — Milestone payment structures, 100% IP/source code ownership transfer, warranty, and liability limits.
12. **Custom 404 (`/_not-found`)** — Clean fallback navigation.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript 5.6 (strict mode)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 3.4 with custom brand tokens
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Validation:** Zod 3.23 (schema validation for inquiries & API routes)
- **Optimization:** Automatic Static Generation (SSG), automated `sitemap.xml` & `robots.txt`
